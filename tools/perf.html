Riptide Rush: art benchmark

Draws 200 mixed enemies, 60 bullets, the player and the arena background through src/art.js, on a 1920x1080 backing canvas (logical 1280x720, scale 1.5). Each frame ends with a 1x1 getImageData so the time includes the canvas raster, not only the JavaScript. The scene is deterministic (fixed 60 Hz simulation step), so before and after runs draw the same frames. Results go to window.__perf after a 5 s measured run, which follows a 120-frame warm-up that fills the sprite caches. Add ?parts=background,enemies,bullets,player to time a subset.
